## My Programming Logbook:

Here’s where I keep my weekly notes as I figure out programming. Every file captures what clicked for me that week :the concepts, the “aha!” moments, the little stumbles, all of it. It’s not just about writing code. It’s about noticing how I’m changing as a programmer.

**What’s Inside:**

- Week 01 : Concerning Programming  
  My first real thoughts about what programming means and why it’s even important.

- Week 02 : A Programming Language Tour  
  I jumped around different languages and started to spot what makes each one unique (or not so unique).

- Week 03 : Computers and Instructions  
  Breaking down how computers follow directions, step by step, and realizing just how careful you have to be.

- Week 04 : A Programmer’s Work  
  Getting to know the tools, the workspaces, and what a normal day in code actually feels like.

- Week 05 : Bugs (and Ducks)  
  My debugging adventures, the mistakes I managed to make, and how talking things through with a rubber duck weirdly helps.

- Week 07 : Handling Errors or How Programming Develops  
  Figuring out how to deal with errors, and looking at how programming itself keeps changing.

- Week 08 : How Does AI Fit In  
  Thinking out loud about where AI shows up in programming and how it’s shaking things up.

- Week 09 : Programming Properly: DRY and WET  
  What it means to write clean code, when to stick to the rules, and when to bend them.

 **Why I’m Keeping This Logbook:**

- To actually see how far I’m getting, week after week.
- To remember the big ideas and the examples that made things click.
- To look back on my mistakes and how I got past them.
- To have something I can flip through later when I need a reminder.

---

I’m writing this logbook in Markdown because simple is best. It’s my way of turning each week’s lessons into something that feels real and sticks with me.
